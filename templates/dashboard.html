Verified ERPNext v16 Architecture & Product Facts (2026)

1. Release Version & Database Stack

Latest Release: ERPNext v16 (v16.0.0, released 12 January 2026 on Frappe Framework v16, alongside v15 LTS). License: GNU GPL v3.

Production Stack: MariaDB 10.6+ (InnoDB, UTF8MB4) with tab* DocType tables (backend/erpnext_mariadb_schema.sql), Python 3.12+, Redis, and Vue 3 Desk.

Local Prototype vs. Live Container: voltedge_ebs.db (SQLite) powers this offline showcase; erpnext_v16_docker/docker-compose.yml runs real frappe/erpnext:v16.0.0 + MariaDB 10.6 on port 8080.

2. Verified Frappe / ERPNext Client Deployments (frappe.io/blog)

Verified Companies: Precihole Sports (precision engineering), Banaraswala Wire Mesh (wire manufacturing), Multicolor Steels (roll-forming steel), Rangsutra (retail & craft supply chain), and Shizuoka General Hospital.

Attribution Note: Zerodha (Rainmatter) is an equity investor in Frappe Technologies, not a documented ERP customer implementation.
