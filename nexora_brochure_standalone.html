3. End-to-End Operational Workflows

A. Order-to-Cash (O2C) & Smart Warehousing

When a buyer approves a quote for 40 units of Servo Actuator #AC-40:

  • CRM converts Quotation → Sales Order and locks inventory in Finished Goods Bin B-04.
  • Warehouse staff scan pallet QR codes using any Android browser to issue the Delivery Challan.
  • Finance posts GST e-Invoice with IRN QR code and reconciles NEFT/RTGS receipts automatically.

B. Procure-to-Pay (P2P) & Manufacturing

When raw castings for Valve Bodies drop below minimum safety stock:

  • MRP raises a Material Request → Buyer approves Purchase Order PO-3041 after vendor rate check.
  • Store clerk logs Goods Receipt Note (GRN-112) and routes 10% sample to Quality Inspection.
  • Work Order consumes released raw castings while capturing CNC telemetry from mqtt://plant-line-b/telemetry.
