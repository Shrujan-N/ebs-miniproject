Procurement Intelligence Agent

Eliminates both production-halting stockouts and excess working-capital tie-up. The agent continuously evaluates multi-echelon warehouse balances against live sales velocity and supplier transit variance.

Uses Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) recurrent neural networks trained on 36 months of historical consumption, seasonal BOM demand, and vendor lead-time distributions to compute multi-horizon time-series forecasts and dynamic depletion rates.

AGENT 02 • MANUFACTURING & QUALITY

Production Optimization Agent

Synchronizes shop-floor execution with real-time machine health and component availability, preventing defective batches from cascading downstream into finished goods assembly.

Core Neural & Edge Specification

Combines low-latency IoT Edge Processing (MQTT protocols) for PLC/sensor telemetry with Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) performing high-speed optical defect recognition via computer vision on assembly lines.

  • 1. Autonomous Perception & Planning: Isolates defective sub-assemblies in real time, evaluates remaining work-center capacity, and computes an updated finite-capacity production schedule.
  • 2. Human Oversight Gate: Major work-order re-prioritizations or line changeovers require explicit approval from the Human Production Manager before PLC dispatch.
AGENT 03 • FINANCE, AP & AUDIT

Financial Guardian Agent

Protects enterprise treasury from duplicate billing, phantom vendor fraud, and tax-code mismatches by auditing 100% of incoming supplier invoices prior to general ledger posting.

Core Neural Specification

Deploys Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for zero-template contextual parsing of complex multi-page invoices and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for relational anomaly detection and automated 3-way matching across Purchase Orders, Goods Receipts, and Invoices.

  • 1. Autonomous Perception & Verification: Extracts line-item quantities, HSN/GST codes, and bank IFSC details via VLM, then traverses the GNN transaction graph to detect circular billing or unit-price drift.
  • 2. Human Oversight Gate: Clean 3-way matches are queued for settlement, while any flagged discrepancy is quarantined for the Human Finance Officer to inspect and authorize or reject.

04 // Human-in-the-Loop Execution

AI-Human Collaboration Workflow: Intelligent Procurement

AI Agent Autonomous Step Human Governance Gate
STEP 01 AI: PERCEPTION

1. Inventory & Demand Sensing

Procurement Agent monitors live bin quantities across warehouses and runs LSTM demand inference to detect an impending component shortfall before safety stock is breached.

Trigger: Depletion < 14d buffer
STEP 02 AI: REASONING

2. Supplier Scoring & PO Draft

Evaluates contracted suppliers on unit price, historical lead-time variance, and quality score; calculates optimal EOQ quantity and generates a complete Purchase Order draft.

Output: Draft PO + Rationale Brief
STEP 03 HUMAN: GOVERNANCE

3. Manager Review & Approval

Human Procurement Manager inspects the AI's vendor rationale and budget impact:
• Approved: Releases PO for dispatch.
• Rejected / Revised: Adjusts quantity or vendor and feeds constraint back to the agent.

Mandatory Gate for PO > ₹50,000
STEP 04 AI: EXECUTION

4. Dispatch, 3-Way Match & Ledger

Once approved, the agent transmits the PO via EDI/API, tracks shipment ASN milestones, updates warehouse stock upon Goods Receipt, and triggers GNN 3-way invoice matching.

Exception Handling → Human CFO
05 // Enterprise Guardrails & Zero-Trust Controls

Autonomous Intelligence. Accountable Decisions.

"NEXORA combines autonomous intelligence with human accountability."

GUARDRAIL 01 // RBAC

Role-Based Access & Agent Scopes

Every AI agent is bound to a least-privilege service identity. The Procurement Agent can draft purchase orders but possesses zero cryptographic permission to alter vendor bank details or release treasury payments.

GUARDRAIL 02 // ENCRYPTION

End-to-End Data Encryption

All transactional tables, customer PII, and supplier contracts are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM and in transit via TLS 1.3. Tenant model weights are strictly isolated with zero cross-company training leakage.

GUARDRAIL 03 // AUDITABILITY

Immutable Audit Trails

Every agent observation, model inference score, prompt context, and human approval signature is written to an append-only cryptographic audit log—ensuring full SOX, ISO 27001, and statutory tax compliance.

GUARDRAIL 04 // CONTAINMENT

Spending Limits & Instant Rollback

Hard deterministic spending caps prevent autonomous execution above board-defined monetary thresholds, paired with one-click transactional state rollback and real-time drift kill-switches.
